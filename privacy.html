Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Master Agent is a personal tool used exclusively by its owner, Thibaud Catry, with his own Google accounts. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent, the app accesses Gmail messages (read, and create drafts) and Google Calendar events of the owner's own accounts, through Google's official APIs.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to provide assistant features to the owner (search, summaries, drafts, scheduling).
  • Access tokens are stored locally in the macOS Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own assistant.
  • Content may be processed by an AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to answer the owner's own requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Master Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
